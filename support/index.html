FAQ

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your email. We’ll send you a reset link. Open it on the device where CareerCrush is installed and it will hand you back to the app to set a new password. Links expire, so request a fresh one if it no longer works.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your profile, photos, likes, and the messages you sent, and ends your matches. If you can’t sign in, email support@careercrush.app from the address on the account and we’ll delete it within 7 days.

If your account is suspended, the app cannot delete it. Email support@careercrush.app from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We delete it within 30 days. Full instructions are on the Delete your account page.

How do I report a user?

On a profile in Discover, tap the … on the photo. In a chat, tap the … in the header, or press and hold their message. Choose Report. Reporting also blocks them. We review every report within 24 hours. For anything urgent or involving safety, email support@careercrush.app with their profile name.
