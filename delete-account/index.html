Delete your account

You can delete your CareerCrush account from inside the app. It takes two steps and happens right away.

Delete it in the app

Step 1

Open CareerCrush, go to Profile, then Settings.

Step 2

Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. We delete your profile, your photos, your likes, your matches, and your messages.

Reports we received about your account may be kept after the account is gone. We keep them for safety — so a banned user cannot come back with a new account — and because we may need them for a legal reason. We keep them only as long as those reasons hold. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Can’t access the app?

Email support@careercrush.app from the address on the account and we will delete it within 7 days.
