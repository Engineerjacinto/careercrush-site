Delete your account

You can delete your CareerCrush account from inside the app. It takes two steps and happens right away.

Delete it in the app

Step 1

Open CareerCrush, go to Profile, then Settings.

Step 2

Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. We delete your sign-in, your profile, your photos, your likes, your blocks, and the messages you sent, except a message that is part of an open report. Your matches end. Messages the other person sent you in those conversations stay hidden for 90 days and are then deleted.

Reports we received about your account may be kept after the account is gone. We keep them for safety, so we have a record if the same person is reported again, and because we may need them for a legal reason. We keep them only as long as those reasons hold. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Can’t access the app?

Email support@careercrush.app from the address on the account and we will delete it within 7 days.

Is your account suspended?

The app cannot delete a suspended account. Email support@careercrush.app from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We delete it within 30 days.
