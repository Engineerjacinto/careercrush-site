Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-28 · Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains what CareerCrush collects, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and how you delete it.

CareerCrush is operated by Anthony Asare and Loudia Merant, two individuals in the United States. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean them. Email us at support@careercrush.app.

What we collect and why

Account

Your email address and password. We use them to sign you in and to send account email, such as the confirmation code and password resets. Your password is hashed by our hosting provider. We never see it and we cannot recover it. We also record which version of the Terms you accepted and when.

Your profile, which other members see

Your first name, your age, gender, city and state, career title, industries, ambition level, relationship goals, your five-year goals, your bio, and your photo.

Your relocation plans: whether you are staying, open to moving, or planning a move, and if you are planning one, the city, state and timeline.

The times you are usually free, such as weekday evenings, and how often you travel.

We use all of this to build your profile, to work out the compatibility score, and to decide whom to show you. The score is calculated each time Discover loads and is not stored.

Private details, which no other member sees

Your birth date, who you are looking for, your preferred age range, and whether you want matches only in your state. Other members see your age, never your birth date.

Your gender together with who you are looking for can reveal your sexual orientation. We use these details only to decide who sees whom.

Photos

Your photo is kept in private storage, not on the open internet. The app loads it through a link that expires after five minutes. Only you, your matches, and people the app may show you to in Discover can load it. When you replace your photo, we delete the old one.

Location

You type your city and state. The app never reads your phone’s location.

What you do in the app

The likes you send, your matches, and the messages you send and receive, including when each was sent and whether it was read.

When you last opened each conversation. We use it only for your unread badge and never show it to the other person.

Which profiles Discover showed you each day. We use it to apply the daily limit.

When you were last active. We use it to order Discover and never show it to anyone.

The members you block, the reports you file and any details you write in them, and reports other members file about you.

Automatic text check

Before a message or profile text is saved, it is checked against a list of words that are not allowed. Text that fails the check is refused and not saved.

Technical logs

Our hosting provider keeps short-lived service logs, such as IP address, request time and app version, to run and secure the service.

What we do not do

We do not use analytics. We do not use crash reporting. We do not show ads and do not use any advertising tool. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

The app does not read your contacts or your location, has no in-app purchases, and does not send push notifications today.

Your messages

Messages are private between matched members. We do not read them. The exception is a report. When someone reports a message or a conversation, we read the reported message and, when needed, the conversation it came from.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and profile while your account exists.

Messages in an active match stay until the match ends. When a match ends, because someone unmatched, blocked, or deleted their account, the conversation disappears for both people right away. We keep those messages for 90 days and then delete them automatically. A message that is part of an open report is kept until the report is closed, then deleted in the next daily cleanup.

When a match ends, we keep a record that the two of you matched and when it ended, without any messages, so the pair cannot be matched again.

We delete the record of which profiles Discover showed you after seven days.

Reports and evidence

A report keeps the reason, any details the reporter wrote, a copy of the reported message, and a snapshot of the reported profile as it was at that moment: display name, career title, bio, and a copy of the profile photo. The photo copy sits in private storage that only we can open. Reports and their evidence are never shown to other members.

We keep reports after either account is deleted, for safety, so we have a record if the same person is reported again, and because we may need them for a legal reason. We keep them only as long as those reasons hold.

If you file a report and later delete your account, the report stays, but your account is no longer linked to it.

Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider’s backups for a short time before those backups are replaced.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm twice. Deletion happens right away.

We delete your sign-in, your profile and private details, your photos, your likes, your blocks, and the messages you sent, except a message that is part of an open report. Your matches end. Messages the other person sent you in those conversations stay hidden for 90 days and are then deleted. Reports stay as described above.

If you cannot sign in, email support@careercrush.app from the address on the account and we will delete it within 7 days.

If your account is suspended, the app cannot delete it. Email support@careercrush.app from the address on the account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your account page.

Safety and moderation

You can report or block any member from Discover or from a chat. Reporting also blocks. We review every report within 24 hours.

We may suspend an account that breaks the Terms. A suspended account cannot sign in, and its profile and matches disappear. We record when an account was suspended.

Who handles your data

We share data only with the companies that run parts of the service, and only what each needs:

  • Supabase. Hosts our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
  • Resend. Sends account email such as the confirmation code and password resets, so it receives your email address and the message. It also sends us an alert for each new report, which contains only the report number, reason and time.
  • ImprovMX. Forwards mail sent to support@careercrush.app to our inbox, so it receives your email address and what you write to us.
  • Google (Gmail). The inbox where we read support mail.
  • Apple. Distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight under its own privacy policy.

We share information with law enforcement or other authorities when the law requires it, such as a valid subpoena or court order, or when we believe someone is in danger.

If the app is ever sold or transferred, your information may go with it. We would tell you first.

Other members see your profile and the messages you send them. They never see your email, your birth date, your private details, your blocks and reports, or when you were last active.

Your rights

You can see and correct your profile in the app.

You can delete your account as described above.

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you. Email us and we will send it.

We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

California residents

If you live in California, the CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, where it comes from, why we use it, and whom we share it with. You also have the right to get a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

In the last 12 months we collected these categories: identifiers (email address, account ID, IP address); your name; age and gender; sensitive personal information (your account login, and sexual orientation as far as it can be inferred from your gender and who you are looking for); the city and state you entered; content you create (profile text, photos, messages); and your activity in the app. All of it comes from you. We use it to run the service and keep members safe. We disclose it only to the service providers listed above.

We do not sell or share personal information, as the CCPA defines those terms, so there is nothing to opt out of. We use sensitive personal information only to provide the service, so the right to limit its use does not apply.

You may use an authorized agent. To make a request, email support@careercrush.app. We verify the request, usually by confirming you control the email address on the account, and respond within 45 days.

Children

CareerCrush is for adults 21 and older. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Sign-up requires a birth date showing you are 21 or older, and our database refuses any account that does not. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 21, we delete it. To tell us about one, use Report and choose Underage, or email support@careercrush.app.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Our hosting provider encrypts stored data. Passwords are hashed. Your sign-in session is stored on your phone in encrypted storage, and its key is kept in the iOS Keychain. Database access rules stop members from reading each other’s private details. Photos load only through links that expire in minutes. Only the operators named above have administrative access, and they use it only when needed.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

When we update this policy, we post the new version on this page with its effective date at the top. If you keep using CareerCrush after that date, you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email support@careercrush.app.
